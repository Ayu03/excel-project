# AI Learning Log: Excel Project

## 1. Project Overview

**Project:** Superstore Sales Analysis (Excel)

**Tool:** Microsoft Excel

**Dataset:** Superstore Sales Dataset (Canada Retail Store)

**Objective:** The objective of this project was to analyze the Corporate customer segment of the Superstore Sales dataset and identify the most profitable and the highest loss-making product subcategories across different regions and provinces. Based on these findings, the goal was to derive meaningful business implications and provide practical business recommendations to support better decision-making.

## 2. Where I Used AI

I primarily used AI to improve the communication of my analysis rather than the analysis itself.

Specifically, I used AI to:

- Clarify a few business terms and concepts whenever required.
- Refine the wording of my observations, insights, and recommendations without changing the meaning.
- Help structure the final project report into a clear and professional format.
- Improve the overall readability and expression of my findings.

The analytical thinking, observations, and conclusions were developed by me through exploring and understanding the dataset. AI mainly acted as an editor and communication partner.

## 3. Where I Did Not Use AI

I intentionally did not use AI for the core analytical work of this project.

This included:

- Understanding the business problem.
- Exploring and analyzing the dataset.
- Identifying meaningful patterns and observations.
- Applying Excel features such as filtering, sorting, conditional formatting, Pivot Tables, and report formatting.
- Drawing conclusions from the data.

I wanted the analysis to come entirely from my own understanding before using AI for refinement.

## 4. What AI Helped Me Learn

While working on this project, AI helped me strengthen my business communication and vocabulary.

Some of the areas where it helped me were:

- Understanding the difference between Observation, Insight, Business Implication, and Business Recommendation.
- Clarifying business concepts such as Profit vs. Profit Margin and Product Portfolio Decisions.
- Learning how to convert analytical observations into business-oriented recommendations.
- Improving the structure and professionalism of report writing.
- Learning how to communicate findings in a concise and business-friendly manner.

## 5. What AI Could Not Replace

One of my biggest learnings from this project was that AI cannot replace analytical thinking or business understanding.

AI can certainly suggest ideas, improve language, and help structure information. However, it cannot understand the complete business context unless it is first provided by the analyst. It also cannot reliably identify the most meaningful insights without human judgment.

Even if AI generates a complete analysis, it still needs to be reviewed, validated, and interpreted by someone who understands the business problem and the data.

For me, AI worked best as a supporting tool rather than a replacement for my own thinking.

## 6. Reflection

This project reinforced my belief that AI is a powerful companion, but not a substitute for analytical thinking.

I found the greatest value in using AI after I had already completed my own analysis. It helped me express my ideas more clearly, improve my business language, and organize my thoughts into a professional report.

Going forward, I want to continue using AI as a thinking partner, someone I can discuss ideas with, validate my reasoning against, and refine my communication through. However, I believe the responsibility for understanding the business problem, analyzing the data, and making sound decisions should always remain with the analyst.
